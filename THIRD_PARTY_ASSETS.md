# Third-party assets

Hearthhold's Unity client uses selected KayKit models. Both source repositories identify the free packs as CC0 and explicitly permit personal and commercial use. Copies of the author's license files are stored beside the imported assets.

## Medieval Hexagon Pack

- Author: Kay Lousberg (KayKit)
- Source: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Medieval-Hexagon-Pack-1.0
- License: CC0 1.0
- Imported format: 13 FBX models and one shared color-atlas PNG
- Use: settlement and defensive-building visuals

The imported files are renamed to Hearthhold building roles. Gameplay names and functions remain original to Hearthhold; the FBX geometry is from KayKit.

## Adventurers Character Pack

- Author: Kay Lousberg (KayKit)
- Source: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
- License: CC0 1.0
- Imported format: three rigged FBX models (Knight, Hooded Rogue, Mage) with four PNG texture atlases
- Use: these rigs provide the visual bases for Guardian, Sky Rider, and Alchemist; the Barbarian and Rogue source rigs are not shipped because the current game does not use them

CC0 does not require attribution, but this notice is retained for provenance and maintenance.

## RPG Character Pack (November 2020)

- Author: Quaternius
- Sources: https://quaternius.com/packs/rpgcharacters.html and the author's https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-rpg-characters release
- License: CC0 1.0; the author's license file is included beside the assets
- Imported subset: Warrior, Ranger, Rogue, Cleric, and Wizard rigged FBX models, with their body and weapon textures
- Use: Vanguard, Ranger, Sapper, Medic, and Summoner visuals with their authored movement, combat/spell, hit, and death animation clips
